Macaroni & Cheese

Description

Macaroni and Cheese is a dish that can bring back childhood memories. It can be a simple quick to make dish that pairs lovingly with many other dishes. This recipe steps away from the store bought boxed mac and cheese. It's just about as quick to make, and offers a unique made from scratch taste.

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces of elbow pasta
  • 1 1/2 cups of Cheez Whiz
  • 1/4 cup whole milk
  • 1/4 teaspoon smoked paparika

Steps

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to a steady boil.
  2. Add in the pasta, cooking for ten minutes, ocasionally stirring.
  3. Drain the pasta, keeping a very small amount of liquid in the pot; if removed pasta from pot to drain, place them back in it.
  4. Add in the Cheez Whiz, folding the pata into it.
  5. If the cheese isn't melting good you can place it back on the stove at a low heat level to help it melt.
  6. After the cheese has melted, add the paparika and stir in the milk.
