Meatloaf

Description

Who doesn't love the taste of a home cooked meatloaf? There's something about the way all of the flavors blend togehter that's just magical. This meatloaf blends a lot of flavors together, while not overpowering any flavor in particular.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. 93% lean ground hamburger
  • 1 envelope Lipton Beefy Onion Soup Mix
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/3 cup plain bread crumbs
  • 1/3 cup water
  • 1/4 cup ketchup, plus extra to put on top
  • 1 tablespoon prepared mustard
  • 2 tablespoons brownsugar

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees, and spray a loaf pan with non-stick baking spray.
  2. In the bowl of a stand mixer place the ground hamburger, then then mix on low until it is broken up.
  3. Add in the egg, water, ketchup, mustard, and brownsugar mixing until all of the hamburger appears moistened.
  4. Add in the Lipton Onion Soup Mix, and bread crumbs. Mix one last time, until all the the dry ingredients are incorporated.
  5. Place the meatloaf mixture into the loaf pan, and bake for 1 hour. Remove from the oven and check that the internal temperature is 165 degrees.
  6. Let rest in the pan for 5 minutes before either removing, or slicing in the pan for serving.
